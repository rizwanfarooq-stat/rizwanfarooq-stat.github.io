Research Profile

I am an applied statistician and interdisciplinary researcher specialising in Bayesian inference, spatial and spatiotemporal statistics, geostatistics, computational statistics, and uncertainty quantification.

My research focuses on developing statistical frameworks for analysing complex spatial and temporal processes, with particular interests in prediction, dependence modelling, uncertainty quantification, environmental risk, and data-driven statistical learning.

I completed my PhD in Applied Statistics at Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad. My doctoral research focused on Bayesian spatiotemporal modelling of climate warming and drought persistence, integrating spatial and temporal dependence, prediction and uncertainty quantification.

Since 2025, I have published five first-author, peer-reviewed research articles involving Bayesian inference, computational statistics, spatial modelling, climate variability, drought dynamics and environmental risk assessment.

Professional Perspective

Alongside academic research, I bring more than 15 years of professional experience in applied statistics and real-world data analysis, including survey methodology, large-scale field operations, data-quality assessment, statistical processing, and evidence generation across health, agriculture, education, employment, socioeconomic and population domains.
